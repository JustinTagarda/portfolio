Privacy Policy for Cognify

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

Cognify is a study application for creating, organizing, generating, and reviewing flashcards and other study content. Cognify is developed by JustinTagarda in the Philippines.

Cognify is designed to be local-first. The app can be used without a Google account, and local SQLite storage remains the working source of truth for study data. Cognify does not use a remote AI service for its core card-generation workflow.

Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, Cognify may store the following in app-private storage:

  • subjects, study sets, cards, draft cards, notes, imported text, and generated study content
  • review history, answers, progress, session summaries, and related study statistics
  • files, PDF-derived content, captured photos, cropped images, and OCR working data used during an import or generation flow
  • audio preferences and locally generated or cached study-session audio where supported
  • theme, storage-mode, OCR-engine, TTS-engine, and other app preferences
  • Google sync metadata, pending-sync records, recovery state, and local working-copy data when online sync is enabled
  • diagnostic logs used to troubleshoot failures and improve reliability; in Cognify's current release configuration, these logs remain on your device unless you separately choose to attach the sanitized diagnostic report described below
  • optional locally installed OCR or TTS runtime files when you choose to install them on a supported platform

Offline Use

You may use Cognify in offline mode without signing in. In that mode, your study content is stored locally on the device and Cognify does not synchronize it to Google Drive.

Local data may remain after signing out or uninstalling, depending on the operating system and its app-data behavior. Clearing Cognify's app data or uninstalling the app is the device-level way to remove local data. You may also need to remove separately installed optional runtime files where applicable.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive Sync

If you choose to sign in and enable online mode, Cognify uses Google services to authenticate you and optionally synchronize your study data between supported devices.

Depending on the sign-in response and features used, Cognify may receive and use:

  • a Google account subject or account identifier
  • your Google account email address and profile/display name
  • your Google profile image URL for account presentation, where supplied by Google
  • authentication credentials or tokens needed to make authorized requests; these are stored and handled by the sign-in integration and are not displayed as study content

Cognify requests the Google Drive appDataFolder scope. It uses that private application-data area for an account-scoped working copy of Cognify data and synchronization metadata. Cognify keeps a local SQLite copy and does not use Google Drive as a live database.

Online sync is optional. If authorization expires or Google Drive is unavailable, Cognify keeps local study data available and may retain pending sync work for a later retry. Google services are also governed by Google's own policies and terms.

On Android, before Cognify attempts silent Google-session restoration or starts an interactive Google sign-in, it performs a short HTTPS availability check to Google's account service. This check helps Cognify avoid starting sign-in when Google cannot be reached. It does not send Cognify study content, feedback content, or Google profile data.

Notes, Files, Photos, OCR, and Card Generation

Cognify can create draft cards from notes, pasted text, supported files, PDFs, scanned documents, camera photos, and cropped images. You choose what content to provide.

OCR processing is intended to run locally on the device. Android may use the bundled ML Kit text-recognition provider. Windows may use Windows OCR or an optional locally installed PP-OCRv6 provider. Optional provider packages and runtime files may be downloaded when you explicitly choose to install them on a supported platform.

OCR output and generated cards may contain the words and information present in your source material. Cognify does not intentionally send the primary OCR or core card-generation content to a cloud OCR or remote AI service.

Speech Recognition and Text-to-Speech

Some study and review flows can use speech recognition and text-to-speech. Android may use an available Android speech-recognition service and Android TextToSpeech engine. Windows may use Windows speech synthesis and, where installed, the optional KittenTTS runtime.

Speech recognition and synthesis depend on the operating system and selected provider. Cognify does not intentionally retain microphone recordings as study content. The operating system or selected third-party speech service may process audio or speech according to its own privacy practices; review the provider's terms and settings before using speech features.

Advertising

The Android version of Cognify may display native advertisements provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive device, advertising, diagnostic, and interaction information, including advertising identifiers, to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize advertising as permitted by applicable settings and law.

Cognify does not sell your personal information. Advertising data is handled by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads privacy guidance for more information.

Feedback

If you use the Feedback screen, Cognify sends the feedback text you enter over HTTPS to a Cognify support relay operated by JustinTagarda using Google Apps Script. The relay delivers the message to the shared Cognify support email inbox at justintagarda.support@gmail.com.

If you are signed in, Cognify shows an Include my email for follow-up option. Your Google account email address is sent with the feedback only when you leave that option selected. If you turn it off, or if you are not signed in, Cognify sends the feedback without an email address as anonymous feedback.

Feedback text is used to respond when contact information is provided and to improve Cognify. Raw diagnostic-log files, study content, attachments other than the optional report described below, Google access tokens, and Google account identifiers other than the optional email address are not sent through Feedback.

The Include diagnostic report option is enabled by default when you send feedback, but you can clear it before sending. When enabled, Cognify creates a small plain-text summary from recent local warnings and errors and attaches it to the feedback email. The report may include the Cognify version/build, platform and operating-system version, fixed diagnostic event IDs, fixed feature/stage/failure classifications, counts, hour-rounded occurrence times, short fingerprints derived only from those safe fields, and sanitized Cognify source call sites. It excludes raw log files, normal-operation entries, raw messages, exception text, stack traces, log contexts, file paths, account details, identifiers, and study content. If no eligible events are available, no report is attached.

Network Activity and Third-Party Services

Cognify may use network access for:

  • Google sign-in, account-profile requests, and authorized Google Drive synchronization
  • optional OCR or TTS runtime downloads that you initiate
  • Android AdMob advertising
  • the optional Feedback submission service described above
  • platform update, licensing, or add-on services where supported by a platform distribution channel

Except for these features and the services required by your operating system or selected provider, Cognify is designed to operate locally. Third-party services may have separate privacy policies, retention practices, and availability rules.

Store Purchases and Add-ons

On supported platforms, Cognify may use the applicable app store to check add-on entitlements and offer optional paid plans. The Windows version currently includes Microsoft Store add-on flows for a lifetime plan and a monthly plan. Android Google Play purchase products are not currently configured.

Cognify does not collect or store your payment-card details. The relevant app store processes payments and may process purchase, subscription, entitlement, device, and account information under its own terms and privacy policy. Cognify may receive the entitlement result needed to enable the purchased capability.

How Information Is Used

Cognify uses information to:

  • create, store, organize, and review your study content
  • generate draft cards and process OCR inputs
  • evaluate answers locally and present study progress
  • provide optional Google authentication, backup, and synchronization
  • provide optional speech-recognition and text-to-speech functionality
  • remember preferences and maintain local app operation
  • diagnose failures and improve reliability through app diagnostics
  • receive, respond to, and improve Cognify using voluntary feedback
  • display and measure advertisements on Android

Sharing and Disclosure

Cognify does not sell personal information and does not intentionally share personal information with third-party advertisers outside the advertising services described above.

Information may be sent to Google when you sign in, request Google profile information, enable Drive sync, or receive AdMob advertising. Information may also be processed by your operating system, Microsoft Store, Google Play, or an optional provider when you use speech, OCR, updates, licensing, or add-on functionality.

When you send feedback, the feedback text and any email address you choose to include are transmitted to the Google Apps Script relay and delivered to the Cognify support inbox. Google provides the Apps Script and email services used for that delivery and handles information under its own policies.

Cognify's source code can support a separately configured remote diagnostic-log endpoint, but the current release configuration does not enable one. If a future release enables remote diagnostic logging, Cognify will update this policy before distribution to identify the data categories sent, recipient, purpose, retention period, and deletion process.

Security

Cognify uses app-private local storage, HTTPS feedback transmission, and authorized Google service access for the features described in this policy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in free-form notes, imported files, photos, or feedback.

Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • use Cognify offline without a Google account
  • choose whether to sign in and enable Google Drive sync
  • sign out of Google and switch back to offline mode
  • create, edit, review, or remove supported study content within the app
  • choose supported OCR and TTS providers and install or remove optional providers where available
  • manage Android permissions and advertising preferences through Android and Google settings
  • choose whether to include your signed-in email address with feedback
  • clear local app data or uninstall Cognify to remove device-local data
  • request deletion of your Cognify account-associated data using the account deletion page

You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or to object to processing of personal information that we control, subject to applicable law and any lawful exceptions. Use the contact details below and identify the relevant Cognify feature or feedback message.

Account Deletion and Retention

If you request deletion, we will verify the request and delete or remove access to Cognify account-associated data under our control, including account-linked Cognify sync data and the Cognify data stored in the Google Drive app-data area where we are able to do so.

Deletion of remote account-associated data does not automatically delete local SQLite data, local logs, cached files, captured photos, or optional OCR/TTS runtime files on your devices. Follow the instructions on the account deletion page for local cleanup.

We may retain limited information when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or to document a deletion request. We do not retain data longer than reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Feedback text and any opted-in contact email are retained only as long as needed to respond to the feedback and improve Cognify, for no longer than 24 months, unless a longer period is required by law or a deletion request is processed earlier.

Children's Privacy

Cognify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when Cognify's features, third-party services, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at this URL, together with its effective date.
