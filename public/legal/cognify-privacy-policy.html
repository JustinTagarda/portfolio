Privacy Policy for Cognify

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Overview

Cognify is a study app for building, reviewing, and generating cards from notes, files, PDFs, and photos.

The app is designed to work locally first using a local SQLite database. Card generation runs through local app logic, and the app does not call a remote AI service for its core card-generation flow.

Information Stored Locally

Cognify may store data locally on the device, including:

  • subjects, study sets, and cards
  • draft cards and card review states
  • notes, imported text, and generated study content
  • selected files, captured photos, and OCR-processed text used during generation
  • app settings such as theme, storage mode, and OCR provider preference
  • online sync metadata and local working-copy data when online mode is enabled
  • diagnostic logs written by the app

Offline Mode

Cognify can be used in offline mode without a Google account.

In offline mode, study data is stored locally on the device and is not synchronized to Google Drive.

Online Mode and Google Services

If you choose online mode, Cognify may use Google sign-in and Google Drive appDataFolder storage to keep an account-scoped working copy of your data and sync it between devices.

When online mode is used, Cognify may process and store information such as:

  • your Google account subject and email address
  • study content you choose to save or sync
  • sync metadata required to keep the active online profile consistent

Cognify does not use Google Drive as a live database. The app stores and manages its own local working copy and sync metadata.

Card Generation and OCR

Cognify can generate draft cards from pasted notes, uploaded files, PDFs, and camera captures.

For image-based and scanned-document workflows, Cognify may use local OCR on the device. On Windows, the app supports Windows OCR and may also support optional locally installed OCR providers.

OCR processing is intended to stay local to your device. The app does not intentionally send OCR content to a cloud OCR service for its primary workflow.

Network Activity

Cognify may use network access for:

  • signing in with Google when online mode is used
  • syncing app data through Google Drive appDataFolder
  • downloading optional local OCR provider files when the user chooses to install them
  • checking for Microsoft Store updates or licensing features when the app is distributed through the Store

Outside of those features, Cognify is designed to operate locally.

Sharing and Disclosure

Cognify does not sell personal information.

Cognify does not share personal information with third-party advertisers.

The app may communicate with Google services only when you choose online mode or sign in with Google, and with Microsoft Store services when applicable to the installed build.

How Information Is Used

Cognify uses stored or transmitted information to:

  • manage subjects, study sets, and cards
  • generate and review study content
  • sync data for users who choose online mode
  • support OCR-based import workflows
  • remember app settings and preferences
  • diagnose errors and improve reliability

Security

Cognify uses local app storage for its offline database and online working copy. Users should avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in free-form notes or imported files.

If you use online mode, your data is associated with your Google account and the app's sync model for that account.

User Controls

Users can:

  • choose offline mode or online mode
  • sign in or sign out of Google
  • manage subjects, study sets, cards, and drafts inside the app
  • change the theme and OCR provider settings where supported
  • remove local app data by deleting the app or clearing app data on the device

Access and Deletion

Users can view, edit, and delete app data inside Cognify where supported.

Uninstalling the app may not automatically remove every local data file, log, or optional OCR runtime artifact. Users who want full cleanup may need to remove app data directories and related local files manually.

Children's Privacy

Cognify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated over time. The latest version should be published at the app's privacy policy URL.
