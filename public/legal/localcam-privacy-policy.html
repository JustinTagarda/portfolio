Privacy Policy for LocalCam

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Overview

LocalCam is a Windows desktop app for discovering cameras on a local network and viewing RTSP video streams in a multi-tile viewer.

The app is designed to operate primarily on the user's device and local network. Most app data is stored locally on the user's Windows device.

Information Stored Locally

LocalCam may store data locally on the device, including:

  • RTSP username and password entered by the user
  • RTSP stream path entered by the user
  • camera discovery data such as IP address, host name, MAC address, open ports, and detection results
  • app settings such as window size, auto-stream preference, and save folder selections
  • snapshot images and recording files saved by the user
  • diagnostic logs written by the app

Network Activity

LocalCam may use network access for:

  • discovering compatible cameras on the user's local network
  • connecting to RTSP video streams on cameras the user has chosen to view
  • checking for Microsoft Store updates when that feature is available

LocalCam does not intentionally upload user settings, credentials, snapshots, recordings, or logs to a third-party service.

How Information Is Used

LocalCam uses stored or transmitted information to:

  • find compatible cameras on the local network
  • connect to and display live RTSP video
  • save snapshots and recordings to folders selected by the user
  • remember app preferences and window state
  • diagnose errors and improve reliability

Sharing and Disclosure

LocalCam does not sell personal information.

LocalCam does not share personal information with third-party advertisers.

The app may communicate with cameras on the user's local network and with Microsoft Store services when update checking or installation is used.

Security

LocalCam stores settings and logs locally on the user's device. RTSP credentials and app settings remain under the user's control through the app's settings screen.

User Controls

Users can:

  • change RTSP credentials, stream path, and save folders in Settings
  • start or stop streams and recordings
  • delete local app data, logs, snapshots, and recordings from their device

Access and Deletion

Users can view and change app settings inside the app where supported. Local data can be removed by deleting the files on the device or uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated over time. The latest version should be published at the app's privacy policy URL.
