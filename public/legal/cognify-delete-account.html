Delete Your Cognify Account

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Cognify account and associated data.

Direct request method:
Email justintagarda@gmail.com with the subject Cognify account deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to justintagarda@gmail.com.
  2. Include the Google account email you used with Cognify.
  3. If you want deletion of a specific device-only offline dataset, say that in the email.

What will be deleted

When we process your request, we will delete or remove access to the Cognify account data we control for that account, including:

  • Google-account-linked sync data stored through Cognify
  • study subjects, study sets, cards, and draft content associated with the account
  • saved settings and sync metadata tied to the account

What may remain on your device

If you used Cognify in offline mode, some local data may remain on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

If you want to remove everything locally, uninstall the app and clear app data from your device settings after the account deletion request is processed.

Timing

We will review deletion requests and process them as soon as reasonably practicable.

Need help?

For questions about deletion or account access, contact justintagarda@gmail.com.
