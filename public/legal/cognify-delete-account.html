Delete Cognify Data for Your Google Account

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete Cognify data associated with the Google account you use in Cognify. Cognify is developed by JustinTagarda. This process does not delete your Google account.

Delete in the app:
  1. Sign in to the Google account whose Cognify data you want to delete and open Google backup details.
  2. Select Delete Cognify data and confirm the deletion.
Need an external deletion request?
Email JustinTagarda

Use the subject Cognify account deletion request. This page and the email pathway are available without reinstalling Cognify.

External deletion requests

  1. Email justintagarda@gmail.com with the required subject.
  2. Include the Google account email used with Cognify so we can identify the relevant sync profile.
  3. Tell us whether you also want instructions for removing offline-only device data, captured files, logs, or optional OCR/TTS runtime files.
  4. Do not include passwords, access tokens, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in the email.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or remove access to Cognify account-associated data under our control, including:

  • the Cognify Google sync profile and related sync metadata associated with the requested account
  • Cognify study subjects, study sets, cards, drafts, review progress, and other study content in the account-linked Google Drive appDataFolder copy
  • account-linked recovery, pending-sync, working-copy records, and the account-linked local Cognify database/profile on the device that performs the in-app deletion

Cognify deletes only its own matching data in Google Drive's private appDataFolder; it does not delete ordinary files in your Google Drive. Google may retain information separately under Google's own policies and legal obligations.

What may remain on your devices

In-app deletion removes the account-linked local Cognify profile on the device used for deletion. It does not automatically remove offline-only or unclaimed local content, diagnostic logs, cached files, captured photos, generated audio, or optional OCR/TTS runtime files.

To remove remaining device-only data, clear Cognify's app data or uninstall Cognify through your device's operating-system settings. Remove separately installed optional runtime files if they remain after uninstalling.

Processing time and limited retention

We will review and process verified requests as soon as reasonably practicable. We may retain limited information when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or to document the deletion request.
